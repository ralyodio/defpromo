Draft posts and replies from your product brief, then review them before you share.

DefPromo keeps your projects, writing variations and local activity history in a browser sidebar. Bring the page's context into a reply, edit the draft, and copy it or fill a supported compose box. You choose whether to publish.

OPEN SPECS, PRACTICAL CONNECTIONS
• OpenProfile: reads a site's /.well-known/openprofile.md and llms.txt before falling back to page scraping, so your product's own description informs the draft.
• OpenConnection: connect through a one-time setup token, with permissions you choose and a connection you can revoke. myna at mynaposter.com is the provider currently integrated by DefPromo.
• NicheDB supplies directory-backed forum suggestions alongside generated subreddit, hashtag and search ideas. Suggestions are places to investigate, not endorsements or permission to promote.

FROM BRIEF TO EDITABLE DRAFT
• Keep separate projects with their URL, audience, features and tone.
• Generate post or comment variations, with optional links and titles where supported.
• Review and edit the page context for a reply.
• Reopen saved variations, copy a draft, or fill a supported form.
• Keep a local record of form fills. Add likes, comments and shares manually to compare your own records.
• Export and import your projects, content, settings and history as JSON.

SETUP AND COSTS
Connect your myna account in Settings using a setup token from https://mynaposter.com/connect. Alternatively, select Your own keys and enter an OpenAI API key. With your own keys, reading a product page that has neither supported site file also requires a configured scraper service. Provider accounts, access and usage charges are separate from the extension; installing DefPromo does not include free AI usage.

CONTROL AND PRIVACY
Projects, settings and history are stored in your browser. Generation sends the relevant project brief and reply context to your selected provider. Product analysis sends the product URL; suggestions can send keywords to NicheDB. If you grant myna activity:write, form-fill activity, text and page URL are also sent to myna. Exported settings can contain credentials: keep backup files private.

DefPromo fills forms; it does not schedule posts or automatically publish them. Its activity counts are not verified posts, reach, follower growth or platform-wide analytics. Check the draft, the destination and each community's rules before sharing. Site changes can affect form filling; copying remains available.
